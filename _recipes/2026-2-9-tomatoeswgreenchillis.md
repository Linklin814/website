# tomatoes & green chillis with other stuff
> **Ingredients**
> 1 cup pasta<br>
> 1 can tomatoes & green chillis<br>
> 1 tbsp lemon juice<br>
> 1 tbsp lime juice<br>
> 1 tbsp black pepper<br>
> 1 tbsp salt (split)<br>
> 1 tbsp garlic powder<br>
> 2 tbsp chilli powder<br>
> 1 tbsp onion powder<br>
> 1 tbsp garlic powder<br>
> 1 tbsp paprika<br>
> 1 tsp cumin<br>
> 1/3 lbs ground beef<br>
> 2 handfuls of spinach
> a dash red chilli flakes (optional)<br>
> 1/4 cup cheese
> dollop of greek yogurt
{: .admonition .info }

## Instructions
1. cook pasta according to box
2. cook ground beef and add spices. cook til its done (duh)
3. once both are done cooking, drain pasta. add both the ground beef and pasta into the pot
5. add can of tomatoes & green chillis to pot as well as lemon and lime juice
6. stir until spinach has wilted
8. boil until its a consistency you like. remember the sauce will be less liquidy as it cools by about half
9. serve and top with cheese and sour cream

## About This Recipe
This recipe is so good in the winter, but can absolutely be a bit spicy. i like to add greek yogurt, but sour cream is probably more traditional.

i have absolutely no idea what to call it. the recipe i adapted it frommcalls it "mexican zucchini beef skillet" which is an attractive name. not sure what makes it mexican other than the cumin.

this recipe used to remind me of a stuffed pepper filling. so i used to make it with pepper and rice. rice is still pretty good in it, but i found the peppers would get soggy.

as always, all seasonings are optional and adjustable. if it's too liquidy you can always boil off some of the water to help.
